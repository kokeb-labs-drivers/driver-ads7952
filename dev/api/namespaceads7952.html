Variable Documentation

◆ kContinuousWord

constexpr bool ads7952::kContinuousWord = true
inlineconstexpr

true: the 16 clocks of a frame come back to back, with no idle SCLK between the two bytes. The chip converts with SCLK (t_conv = 16 SCLK, section 7.9; section 8.1) and the datasheet specifies no conversion with the clock stopped inside a frame.

◆ kCsHoldNs

constexpr uint32_t ads7952::kCsHoldNs = 0
inlineconstexpr

Last SCLK edge to CS high: section 7.9 gives no minimum; CS may rise once the 16th SCLK falling edge has passed (section 8.1).

◆ kQuietNs

constexpr uint32_t ads7952::kQuietNs = 40
inlineconstexpr

Quiet time from SDO going to 3-state (at most 26 ns after the 16th SCLK falling edge, t_d3) to the next CS falling edge, t_q (section 7.9). With CS high for kMinCsHighNs the 16th falling edge must come at least kSdoReleaseNs + kQuietNs before the next CS falling edge.

◆ kSdoReleaseNs

constexpr uint32_t ads7952::kSdoReleaseNs = 26
inlineconstexpr

Longest delay from the 16th SCLK falling edge to SDO in 3-state, t_d3 at +VBD = 1.8 V (section 7.9).

◆ kSpiMode

constexpr uint8_t ads7952::kSpiMode = 0
inlineconstexpr

SPI mode 0: CPOL 0, CPHA 0. SDI is latched on the rising SCLK edge and SDO changes on the falling edge (section 8.1, Figure 2).
