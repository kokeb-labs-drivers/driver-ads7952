Detailed Description

The two alarm thresholds of one channel, in 12-bit result codes. The chip keeps the upper 10 bits and compares them with the upper 10 bits of each result (Table 13), so the two lowest bits of a threshold are dropped. AlarmThreshold{} is the reset state: no alarm.
