◆ Error

enum class ads7952::Error : uint8_t
strong

Why a call failed. Every call of the driver returns one of these in its Result.

Values 0 to 9 are the shared base set; chip-specific values start at 16. The type is [[nodiscard]]: a call whose Error is dropped does not compile quietly (KB-CPP20-037).

Enumerator
Ok 

The call succeeded.

NotInitialized 

EnsureInitialized() has not succeeded, or Deinitialize() ran since.

InvalidArgument 

An argument or a Config field is out of range; nothing was sent.

Bus 

The port's Transfer() (or EnsureInitialized()) returned false.

Timeout 

Not returned by this driver: no call waits for the chip.

DeviceIdMismatch 

At start-up, the replies do not carry the channel addresses the chip must send back (MISO stuck low or carrying a pattern, SDI not reaching the chip, another part on the chip select).

Crc 

Not returned by this driver (the chip sends no CRC).

Busy 

Not returned by this driver (the chip is never busy to a frame).

DeviceFault 

Not returned by this driver (the chip reports no faults).

Unsupported 

A setting the configured part or package lacks (GPIO1 to GPIO3 on the VQFN package), or SetRange() while GPIO2 is the range input.

NoDevice 

At start-up, every reply was 0xFFFF: nothing drives MISO (no chip on the chip select, MISO pulled or floating high).

ChannelMismatch 

A reply that must carry a channel's result carries another address: a frame was lost, the chip was reset, or the bus is broken. The result was not returned.

SequenceMismatch 

The replies of an Auto-1 or Auto-2 scan are not one pass of the programmed sequence: the chip left the mode, lost its program register (a reset or brown-out), or a frame was lost.

WriteMismatch 

Verify::ReadBack found that the chip did not take the setting: the GPIO levels read back differ, or the sequence scanned is not the one just programmed.

RangeMismatch 

The scale monitor (Config::monitor) reads what the other range would give: the chip is not converting in Config::range. GPIO2 is the range input and the board drives it to the other level, or the chip was reset (back to Range 1) and the driver has not seen it.

ScaleMismatch 

The scale monitor reads outside its window in both ranges: the reference is missing, wrong or not yet settled, or the monitor input is not the voltage Config::monitor declares.

DeviceReset 

The chip was reset behind the driver (a brown-out): a reply came from channel 0, the power-on channel (section 8.4.3), where the driver expected another, or the scale monitor found Range 1, the reset value. The registers hold their reset values (no alarm function, no GPIO outputs, the reset alarm thresholds); the driver is uninitialised and EnsureInitialized() programs the chip again.

◆ Variant

enum class ads7952::Variant : uint8_t
strong

Which 12-bit member of the family is fitted. The chip cannot tell, so it is configured.

The four parts share the frame format, the modes and the registers; they differ in the number of channels, which sets the reset value of the Auto-1 mask and the Auto-2 last channel and the number of alarm groups (section 8.4.1, Tables 4 and 12). The 10-bit (ADS7954 to ADS7957) and 8-bit (ADS7958 to ADS7961) parts are not supported.

Enumerator
Ads7950 

4 channels.

Ads7951 

8 channels.

Ads7952 

12 channels. The default.

Ads7953 

16 channels.

Extra verification for one call, chosen per call (handbook KB-ARCH-041).

The datasheet needs none of it: Verify::None, the default, sends only the frames the chip needs. Verify::ReadBack costs frames on the bus; each call that takes it says how many.

Enumerator

ReadBack
