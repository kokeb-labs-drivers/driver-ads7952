Changelog¶

What changed in each release of the driver. Entries under Breaking need source changes in code that used the affected API.

[Unreleased]¶

Changed¶

  • The repository moved to kokeb-labs-drivers; URLs follow.
  • The Hardware page draws the chip from the new docs/hardware.yml: the TSSOP-38 pinout with each pin's port hook, the functional block diagram of section 8.2, the typical application of Figure 60 with its reference, supply and input capacitors, and the pin and parts tables, every fact with its datasheet section and page. The example supply voltages and the reference's own supply are marked as not confirmed. The hand-written pin table is gone.
  • The hardware-lessons-review skill and tools/preflight.sh follow the template.

[0.1.0] - 2026-11-09¶

Breaking¶

  • A reply the driver cannot account for now leaves a running driver uninitialised: after ChannelMismatch, SequenceMismatch, the new Error::DeviceReset (22), a WriteMismatch from a sequence read-back, or a failed CheckScale() (other than Bus), every call returns NotInitialized until EnsureInitialized() succeeds again. A mismatching reply from channel 0, the power-on channel (section 8.4.3), and a CheckScale() that finds Range 1 where the driver sends Range 2 are DeviceReset instead of ChannelMismatch, SequenceMismatch or RangeMismatch. Handle DeviceReset and call EnsureInitialized() after any of these errors.
  • ApplyConfig() on a running driver writes every register, changed or not (6 + 10 frames per alarm group, 36 on the ADS7952), instead of the differences from its own copy.

Added¶

  • IsConfigUncertain(): true after a setter or ApplyConfig() failed with Bus, or a Verify::ReadBack did not confirm the write (the frame may have reached the chip), until every register is written again: ApplyConfig(GetConfig()), or Deinitialize() then EnsureInitialized(). GetConfig() keeps the values last written successfully; alarm thresholds are never assumed set after a failed write.
  • Config::park_channel (opt-in, kNoPark by default): after ReadChannel(0) the chip rests on this channel, so a reset while it would otherwise rest on channel 0, the power-on channel, shows as DeviceReset at the next read. ReadChannel(0) costs one frame more (4 when repeated).
  • First version of the driver for the TI ADS7950, ADS7951, ADS7952 and ADS7953 12-bit SPI SAR ADCs, written on the driver template against datasheet SLAS605C.
  • ads7952::ADS7952<Port> with the configuration in the constructor and one start-up call, EnsureInitialized(): two frames that end any open program sequence, a register reset through the GPIO program register, every program register written, and an identity check that selects two channels and demands their addresses back (a, a, b), which a stuck or patterned MISO, an echo and a deaf SDI cannot pass. ApplyConfig() writes only what changed; Deinitialize() powers the chip down.
  • Conversions keyed on the channel address of every reply: ReadChannel() (1 to 3 frames), ReadChannels() (pipelined, list length + 2 frames), ReadAuto1() and ReadAuto2() (one frame per channel while the sequence runs), TraceFrames() for bring-up. Error::ChannelMismatch and Error::SequenceMismatch.
  • Settings: SetRange() (the next read skips the old-range reply), SetAuto1Channels(), SetAuto2LastChannel(), SetAlarmThresholds(), SetGpioOutputs(), ReadGpio(); Verify::ReadBack per call reads back the GPIO levels or scans the new sequence (Error::WriteMismatch).
  • Config with the reset value of every register, the variant, the package, the reference and the lowest guaranteed +VA (va_min_mv); a constexpr Validate() (Range 2 only while 2 x VREF <= va_min_mv), ResetConfig(), CodeToMicrovolts() (one LSB is full scale / 4096); Kconfig options for the part, package, range, voltages and sequences.
  • A scale monitor: Config::monitor declares a channel with a voltage derived from REFP. EnsureInitialized() checks it once (3 frames), CheckScale() on demand, and CheckScaleCode() judges a code already read, such as the monitor's entry of each Bank, with no frame. Error::RangeMismatch (the chip converts in the other range: a GPIO2 strap or a reset) and Error::ScaleMismatch (the reference or the monitor input is wrong). Validate() refuses a monitor that cannot tell the two ranges apart.
  • Bank::range and ToMicrovolts(bank, channel): a scan always converts with the range it was made in.
  • The "Reference, range and supplies" page: how a code becomes a voltage, choosing VREF and the range for a supply, the scale monitor, ratiometric sensors decoded against their measured supply, power and layout from the datasheet, a board checklist and a worked example of a 5 V sensor board.
  • After a failed frame inside a program sequence the driver repeats the Auto-1 mask or closes the alarm sequence, so the chip never takes the next command as data (found by the bus-fault sweep and the fuzzer).
  • Host tests over a protocol model of the four parts with the bus and board faults a real board can produce, every frame of every operation failed in turn; a libFuzzer target with its corpus replayed as a CTest; the host and ESP32 examples; the frame-pipeline figure. Not verified on hardware.
  • docs/limitations.md: the design choices with the datasheet section each rests on, the limits of the design, what the model cannot show, and the bench steps to run before depending on the driver.

Changed¶

  • The misra-review, cpp20-profile and new-driver-from-template skills and docs/repo-setup.md follow the template: reading the MISRA report, the known tool misfires, enumeration ranges without a sentinel, and the settings tool.
  • tools/preflight.sh and the verify and hardware-lessons-review skills follow the template: every check CI runs that a workstation can run, in one command, and the rules for device resets, faults the chip clears itself, dropped writes, switch-offs that no uncertainty refuses and a model that changes state on its own.
  • The port concepts match each hook's address against exactly one noexcept signature with detail::ExactHook<Port, Sig>::Take(&Port::Hook) instead of probing it with a converting detail::Exactly<T>: no conversion operator is left. A misdeclared hook is still a compile error. kAlwaysFalse takes a class and the enumeration helpers an enumeration (ClassType, Enumeration), each with a compile-fail test. The cpp20-profile skill is the template's again.
  • The repository documents only this driver against its datasheet: each design choice cites the datasheet section it rests on (or states its reasoning and a bench step), with no dated history. The copyright holder is The driver-ads7952 authors in LICENSE, every source header, driver.yml (authors) and the docs footer. tools/check_standalone.py, from the driver template, checks both in the repository checks.
  • The driver follows the current handbook rules and the C++20 profile, from the driver template:
  • Every header has an #ifndef include guard named after its file (KB-CPP-019); no #pragma is left.
  • The bus requirements are constexpr in inc/ads7952_spi_interface.hpp (KB-CPP-123): kSpiMode 0, kMsbFirst, kMaxClockHz 20 MHz, kMinCsHighNs 20 (t_w1), kCsSetupNs 8 (t_su1), kCsHoldNs 0, kQuietNs 40 (t_q), kSdoReleaseNs 26 (t_d3), kContinuousWord and kFrameBits 16, from datasheet sections 7.3, 7.9 and 8.1. The host and ESP32 ports check their settings with static_assert, and the ESP32 port refuses a clock above the limit and drives CS high before it enables the pin (KB-HW-018).
  • SpiPort checks each hook's exact parameter types, noexcept and bool result (KB-CPP20-006): a port whose Transfer() takes a narrower length or is not noexcept no longer compiles. DelayUs and Log are found by the named concepts HasDelayUs and HasLog, and a hook declared with another signature is a compile error (KB-CPP20-007). Compile-fail tests for each case.
  • Error is [[nodiscard]] (KB-CPP20-037), and so are the pure helpers. GetConfig() is &-qualified and deleted on a temporary driver (KB-CPP20-044).
  • Every target builds as ISO C++20 (CMAKE_CXX_EXTENSIONS OFF, -pedantic-errors, the C++20 deprecations as errors); ads7952_header_checks compiles each public header alone on GCC, Clang and the Cortex-M cross-build, and examples/esp32/main/ads7952_cxx20_check.cpp does the same inside the ESP-IDF build. The tests and fuzzers build with the standard library's assertions.
  • Bit work is in uint32_t, with every shift count masked or proved below the width (KB-CPP20-020, -021): no unsigned long or UL shifts (64 bits on the host, 32 on the targets), no promoted uint8_t or uint16_t operand of a shift, ~, | or ^, and no compound assignment that narrows (KB-CPP20-022). <bit> counts and rotates the channel masks (std::popcount, std::rotr, std::countr_zero).
  • ToString(Error) is a switch over every enumerator; Validate() checks enumerations with detail::IsEnumerator(); loops have no extra flag in their condition. The frame builders, the GPIO and mode encoders (now free constexpr functions), the reset configurations, the channel helpers, the identity channels and the code-to-voltage conversion are proved with static_assert against the datasheet tables.
  • The two frame buffers belong to the driver object instead of the call's stack.
  • The chip model (tests/mock_ads7952.hpp) includes no driver header: it has its own frame format, reset values, channel counts and power-up time with their datasheet sections, and tests/test_model.cpp checks them against the driver's at compile time (KB-TST-030).
  • TraceFrames() documents its side effect (the chip is left in Manual mode on the channel); the API guide lists the raw access, and the Status page lists the handbook rules the chip gives no handle for (no clear-on-read flag to latch, no ready bit, 0x0000 being a valid channel 0 reply).
  • The template's review skills (cpp20-profile, misra-review, hardware-lessons-review), the safety-reviewer agent, the updated verify skill and the Handbook source checks CI job, pinned at the handbook's C++20-profile commit; _config/.clang-tidy follows the handbook.

  • The frame cost of every call is unchanged.

  • CI runs fewer jobs, from the driver template: the host tests build GCC and Clang in one job; DCO, the org standard, Markdown and YAML lint, the handbook rules, template drift and cppcheck are steps of one Repository checks job (ci-repo-checks.yml, replacing ci-handbook.yml, ci-cpp-analysis.yml, ci-markdown-lint.yml and ci-yaml-lint.yml); cross-compile and host quality use ci-tools' one-job layouts. No check is dropped. The workflows have no manual (workflow_dispatch) trigger any more.

  • MISRA C++:2023 conformance: the findings of the first scan are fixed, with no change in behaviour or frame cost. A failed read clears its codes with std::fill, whose result is void (Rule 0.1.2); a narrow operand meets a U literal, and a bitwise operator on two uint16_t constants works in uint32_t (Rule 7.0.5); an integer constant argument has the parameter's type through the _u8, _u16 and _u32 literals of ads7952_detail.hpp, which reject an out-of-range value at compile time (compile-fail test), and a narrow result that initialises a uint32_t is cast (Rule 7.0.6); comparisons and arithmetic inside &&, ||, ?: and comparisons are parenthesised (Rule 8.0.1); loop bounds are const locals (Rule 9.5.1); every switch over an enumeration ends with default: break; and keeps its fallback, and the host tests build with -Wswitch-enum so a new enumerator still warns (Rule 9.4.2); modeFrame() has the same parameter name in its declaration and definition (Rule 13.3.3); the .ipp include of ads7952.hpp carries the deviation marker MD-CPP-009 (Rule 19.0.3). clang-tidy's modernize-use-ranges is off: it would bring the discarded std::ranges results back.

Fixed¶

  • A reset behind the driver's back (a brown-out: every register back to its reset value, section 8.4.3, Table 11, section 8.5.3, and an invalid first conversion, section 7.6) is detected wherever a reply can show it, and the driver no longer goes on as if nothing happened. The frame that returns a ReadChannel() result is the Manual word for the channel with the range and GPIO levels, not a "continue" word (same frame count), so the range comes back at the next read. The replies a new select, a ReadChannels() list or an Auto entry discards must carry the Manual channel the chip had settled on. Each frame of an Auto-1 or Auto-2 pass is the Auto word with DI11 = 1 and no restart instead of a "continue" word (same frame count): it re-sends the range, and after a reset it makes the chip enter the mode with the reset sequence, so a sequence of channel 0 alone shows the reset too. ApplyConfig(GetConfig()) restores the whole configuration, the alarm function and thresholds included. A reset at any moment the chip rests on channel 0 in Manual mode (in any mix of reads, without Config::park_channel) shows in no reply, and up to two wrong results can be returned after a reset before it is seen; the limits and the interlock guidance are in docs/limitations.md.
  • The chip model can reset on its own before a chosen frame (brownout_before_frame), with the conversion in flight kept or lost; tests/test_reset.cpp covers the reset on every read path and the alarm interlock, and the fuzzer checks that every mismatch leaves the driver uninitialised.
  • readPipelined(), which indexes the caller's spans, checks their sizes itself (KB-CPP20-043); before, only its caller did.
  • AllChannels() and detail::Auto2Mask() shifted unsigned long, which is 64 bits on the host and 32 on the targets, so a width bug could not show in the host tests; they shift uint32_t now.
  • ValidateGpio() and the alarm writer narrowed in |= (uint8_t |= unsigned); they collect in uint32_t.
